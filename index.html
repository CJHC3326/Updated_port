About

Bridging networks, systems, and the interfaces that bring them to life.

I'm Carl Crisostomo, a network engineer building my career around understanding how systems connect, communicate, and perform. I'm interested in the fundamentals — how traffic moves, why a link drops, how routing decisions are made, and what it takes to keep a network reliable and predictable.

I enjoy working close to the systems themselves, whether that's troubleshooting a routing issue in the terminal, configuring switches, analyzing network behavior, or building labs to understand how everything works together. For me, good engineering starts with understanding what happens beneath the surface.

That combination is where I want to grow — bridging networks, systems, and the interfaces that bring them to life. I'm still early in my career, but I'm building deliberately, one lab, one ticket, and one project at a time.
